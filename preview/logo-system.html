The lit window

Corrected. The pane was an 8.2px square that fell short of both the mullion and the frame's inner edge, leaving hairline gaps, with a corner radius that did not match the frame. It is now clipped to the frame's exact inner shape and extends under the stroke, so the quadrant fills solidly at every size. Each instance needs a unique clip id.

A window with one pane lit. Says someone's there and the door's worth knocking on, without claiming to be an institution. The lit pane is also a working part of the interface, not just decoration — more on that below.

The part that earns its keep

The pane is the status light

Same window, different pane colour, and you have a status indicator that's already the brand. Use it on listing cards, county tiles, and the alert emails — no separate icon set, and people learn the vocabulary from the logo before they read the legend.

Keep the crimson pane for the logo only. The moment crimson also means a status, the mark stops being a mark. Crimson = the brand and the buttons; green, amber and grey = information.
