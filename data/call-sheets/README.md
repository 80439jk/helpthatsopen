# Call sheets

Generated by `scripts/build_call_list.py`. No database needed — open in Sheets, fill
the capture columns, hand back. Rows import as `status_log` entries.

- `week-01-ceap-launch-counties.csv` — **start here.** The 16 CEAP providers serving the
  ten launch counties (18.4M people, 59% of Texas). A day of dialing.
- `week-01-ceap.csv` — all 43 CEAP providers statewide, same ordering.

Ranked by the cold-start priority score from `docs/02-data-model.md`: with nothing
verified, staleness and demand are null, so the score reduces to reach + volatility +
contactability. That is why the Harris County providers sit at the top.

## Columns

Left of `status` is context for the call — do not edit. From `status` rightward is
capture, in the order `docs/05-va-operations.md` asks for it:

| Column | Script step |
|---|---|
| `status` | 2 — never a yes/no question. "How far into the month is the funding lasting?" |
| `funding_lasts_until`, `reopens_on` | 2 |
| `how_to_apply`, `hours`, `daily_cap`, `documents_required` | 3 — read prefilled facts back as statements |
| `most_common_turnaway` | 4 — verbatim, in their words. The highest-value field on the sheet. |
| `anything_changing` | 4 |
| `spoke_with`, `verify_outcome`, `note`, `called_at`, `va` | 5 and wrap |

**Blank beats guessed.** If it wasn't said, leave it empty and put the reason in `note`.
A guessed field poisons the freshness claim; an empty one costs nothing.

`needs_source_verification` is `true` on every row: the org name, phone, and counties
came from a model reading a PDF. Confirm the phone connects to the right organization
before trusting the rest of the row.
